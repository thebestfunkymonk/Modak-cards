# Reference sources

The reference images are the copyright of their creators and are not licensed for redistribution, so they are not in this repository. `refs/hiero/` and `refs/helveticards/` are git-ignored. Download the images from the links below into those folders, using the listed filenames, if you want them locally.

## Hiero Display (`refs/hiero/`)

- Project: [Hiero Display Typeface](https://www.behance.net/gallery/111790007/Hiero-Display-Typeface) by Scott Newland, Behance, 2021. All rights reserved.
- Files: the 16 project images, in page order, from the Behance image CDN (`mir-s3-cdn-cf.behance.net/project_modules/…`). Local filenames keep the CDN asset name, for example `hiero_01_5dc5cc111790007.62d7087df2d1c.png`.
- What we took from it: building glyphs from a small set of primitives (circle, 3×7 rectangle, triangles), the pillowy extruded look, visible seams between primitives, and the solid-versus-wireframe contrast.

## Helveticards (`refs/helveticards/`)

Helveticards by Ryan Myers ("Swiss Style. International Fun."). The original site, helveticards.ryanmyers.me, is offline, so these come from press coverage and Wayback Machine captures.

The `rocious_*` images accompany the post [Helveticards](https://blog.rocious.com/2010/05/helveticards/) on Ferocious Quarterly, May 2010.

What we took from it: the minimal white card, the top-left index with a pip grid, the huge rank numeral that bleeds off the edge into a solid bottom band, the inverted index in the band, and a red back with a wordmark and a cross.
